## Editorial decisions

- The warehouse era credits IBM (1988), Inmon, Kimball and Teradata rather than one "father".
- Separating storage and compute is attributed to BigQuery and Snowflake. We make no claim about Redshift's architecture history.
- The lakehouse is presented as a pattern that emerged across vendors. Databricks popularised the name.
- Comparator ratings are simplified trade-offs, not benchmarks, and the page says so.
