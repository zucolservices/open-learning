## Editorial decisions

- Prices live in `data.ts` (`PRICE`) and in the lifecycle step. Update them together when AWS pricing changes.
- The rename simulation's timing is illustrative; request and byte counts follow the copy + delete model.
- "Real directories" mode groups HDFS and hierarchical namespaces (ADLS, GCS HNS); S3 Express One Zone is covered separately because its rename is single-object only.
