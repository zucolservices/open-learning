## Decisions

- The lab models a date-partitioned table without deletion vectors or row-level concurrency, where the documented rules are simplest; those features are explained in "Fewer conflicts".
- Exception names are shown only where the docs tie them clearly to the case. For OPTIMIZE losing to an UPDATE, the lab says it fails without naming an exception.
- "ADD COLUMN after a concurrent data-only commit succeeds" follows Delta's check order (metadata changes are checked on the winner's commit); not stated verbatim in the docs.
- Also corrected in the object-storage module: S3 conditional writes haven't replaced DynamoDB for open-source Delta Spark, and Delta on Azure does rely on rename-without-overwrite.
