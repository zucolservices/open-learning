# Sources: Naming (fact-checked 2026-10-11)

- Hofmeister, Siegmund & Holt, "Shorter identifier names take longer to comprehend", SANER 2017: 72 professional C# developers; words ~19% faster than letters and abbreviations (no significant difference between those two).
- Lawrie, Morrell, Feild & Binkley, "What's in a Name? A Study of Identifiers", ICPC 2006: full words best, often no significant difference from abbreviations; single letters worst.
- "Two hard things" quote: usually credited to Phil Karlton (Netscape); earliest record is Tim Bray's blog (Dec 2005), recalling c.1996–97; Fowler, "TwoHardThings".
