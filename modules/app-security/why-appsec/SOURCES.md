# Sources: Why application security (fact-checked 2026-10-07)

- US GAO, GAO-18-559 "Data Protection: Actions Taken by Equifax and Federal Agencies in Response to the 2017 Breach" (30 Aug 2018): 13 May 2017 entry, 76 days, ~9,000 queries, unencrypted credentials file.
- House report: the dispute application needed 3 databases but could reach 48.
- US House Committee on Oversight and Government Reform, "The Equifax Data Breach" (Dec 2018): monitoring device inactive 19 months due to an expired certificate (GAO says about 10 months); renewed 29 Jul 2017.
- Apache Struts S2-045 / CVE-2017-5638 (7 Mar 2017); US-CERT notice to Equifax (8 Mar 2017).
- US FTC, Equifax settlement (22 Jul 2019): at least $575 million, potentially up to $700 million; about 147 million people.
- Verizon 2026 Data Breach Investigations Report (19 May 2026): exploitation of vulnerabilities 31%, phishing 16%, credential abuse 13%, third-party involvement 48%.
- IBM Cost of a Data Breach Report 2026 (29 Jul 2026): global average USD 4.99M; India INR 255M (3 Aug 2026).
- NIST CSRC glossary: vulnerability, threat, risk. Larry Smith, "Shift-left testing", Dr. Dobb's (2001).
- The "100x cost to fix" figure is avoided: no traceable IBM Systems Sciences Institute study (The Register, Jul 2021).

The attack chain in step 2 is a simplification of the GAO and House findings.
