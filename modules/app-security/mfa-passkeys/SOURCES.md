# Sources: MFA and passkeys (fact-checked 2026-10-07)

- NIST SP 800-63B-4 (July 2025): SMS/voice OTP as the "restricted" authenticator; phishing resistance "without relying on the vigilance" of the subscriber; manually entered OTPs not phishing-resistant.
- Microsoft Threat Intelligence (12 Jul 2022): AiTM phishing campaign (Evilginx2) targeting more than 10,000 organisations.
- Uber security update (Sept 2022): contractor account, repeated MFA approval requests.
- Group-IB, 0ktapus (2022). CISA, "Implementing Phishing-Resistant MFA" (Oct 2022).
- Apple, Google and Microsoft press release (5 May 2022). W3C WebAuthn Level 3 Recommendation (25 Aug 2026). FIDO Alliance (7 May 2026): ~5 billion passkeys in use.
- Microsoft Authenticator number matching enforced from 8 May 2023.
- RBI Directions on Authentication Mechanisms for Digital Payment Transactions (25 Sep 2025; effective 1 Apr 2026).

The relay attack is a simplified, rules-based story; domains are .example placeholders.
