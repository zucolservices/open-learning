# Sources: Capstone, securing a payments app (fact-checked 2026-10-07)

- PCI DSS v4.0.1 (Jun 2024; newer requirements mandatory 31 Mar 2025): Req 6.4.3 (payment-page script inventory and integrity), Req 11.6.1 (weekly change/tamper detection). SAQ A update (30 Jan 2025).
- RBI card-on-file tokenisation (effective 1 Oct 2022): merchants/gateways may not store actual card numbers. RBI Digital Payment Security Controls (Feb 2021; 2026 Directions for banks).
- British Airways 2018 Magecart: ICO £20M (16 Oct 2020), ~429,612 people, skimmer live 15 days.
- Ticketmaster UK 2018: ICO £1.25M (13 Nov 2020), third-party chat-bot.
- Snowflake customer breaches 2024 (UNC5537): ~165 orgs, stolen credentials, accounts without MFA.
- Coinbase 2025: bribed overseas support contractors; estimated $180–400M in costs.

PayLite and the five incidents are illustrative, each modelled on the cited cases.
