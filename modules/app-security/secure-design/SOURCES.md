# Sources: Secure design principles (fact-checked 2026-10-07)

- Jerome H. Saltzer & Michael D. Schroeder, "The Protection of Information in Computer Systems", Proc. IEEE 63(9), Sept 1975: eight design principles, described as warnings.
- NSA, "Defense in Depth" (undated paper, c. 2000), adapting a military concept.
- OWASP Top 10:2025: A06 Insecure Design (A04 in 2021); A10 Mishandling of Exceptional Conditions (covers failing open, CWE-636).
- CISA, FBI, NSA and partners, "Shifting the Balance of Cybersecurity Risk: Principles and Approaches for Secure by Design Software" (13 Apr 2023); Secure by Design Pledge (8 May 2024, 68 companies; 387 signers listed 7 Oct 2026). Voluntary.
- NIST SP 800-207, Zero Trust Architecture (Aug 2020).
