# Sources: Test-driven development (fact-checked 2026-10-11)

- Kent Beck, _Test-Driven Development: By Example_ (Nov 2002; copyright 2003). Beck, "Canon TDD" (11 Dec 2023, newsletter.kentbeck.com): test list; one test; make it pass, adding discoveries to the list; optionally refactor; repeat. Mistakes listed include converting the whole list to tests up front, tests without assertions, deleting assertions to go green, and mixing refactoring into "make it pass".
- Nagappan, Maximilien, Bhat & Williams (Empirical Software Engineering, 2008): four teams (3 Microsoft, 1 IBM), pre-release defect density 40–90% lower; 15–35% more initial development time (management estimates); not randomised.
- Fucci et al. (IEEE TSE 2017): test-first vs test-last ordering had no important influence; granularity and uniformity (small steady steps) did. Rafique & Mišić (IEEE TSE 2013) meta-analysis: small quality gain, no clear productivity effect.
