# Storyboard: Why test

1. **One line on a Friday** (scroll story): a one-line student discount with a rounding bug; customers find it; replay with a test that fails in a second.
2. **Where is the bug found?** ⭐ (simulation): switch on unit, integration and end-to-end tests; three made-up bugs move from "a customer" to "your laptop" or "CI".
3. **What tests give you, and what they don't** (explore): regressions, safe change, documentation, design feedback; not a proof (Dijkstra).
4. **When bugs escape** (explore): Ariane 5, Knight Capital, CrowdStrike, each a gap in testing.
5. **Fact or myth?** (checkpoint `testing-why`).
6. **What to remember** (wrap).
