# Sources (fact-checked 2026-09-28, before building)

Full notes: scratchpad `agile/m02-facts.md`.

- agilemanifesto.org: title, lead-in, four values, closing line and notice, verbatim; agilemanifesto.org/principles.html: the twelve principles, verbatim (American spelling kept; principle 10's double hyphens shown as em dashes).
- agilemanifesto.org/history.html (Jim Highsmith): the meeting at The Lodge at Snowbird, Utah, 11–13 Feb 2001; the methods represented; "We embrace documentation, but not hundreds of pages of never-maintained and rarely-used tomes"; "We plan, but recognize the limits of planning in a turbulent environment"; dislike of "Light".
- Robert C. Martin's September 2000 invitation ("all the lightweight method leaders in one room").
- Martin Fowler, "Writing The Agile Manifesto" (2006): "agile… captured the adaptiveness and response to change"; values written at the meeting, principles over "the following couple of months".
- Dave Thomas, "Agile is Dead (Long Live Agility)" (4 Mar 2014): "Agile is not a noun, it's an adjective"; "seventeen middle-aged white guys".
- Andy Hunt, "The Failure of Agile" (6 May 2015); Ron Jeffries, "Developers Should Abandon Agile" (10 May 2018); Martin Fowler, "The State of Agile Software in 2018" (25 Aug 2018): "faux-agile", "Agile Industrial Complex".
- The six principle themes are this module's own grouping, labelled as such.
