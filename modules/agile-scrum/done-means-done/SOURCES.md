# Sources (fact-checked 2026-09-28, before building)

Full notes: scratchpad `agile/m16-facts.md`.

- Scrum Guide 2020: DoD "a formal description of the state of the Increment when it meets the quality measures required for the product"; items not meeting it "cannot be released or even presented at the Sprint Review… returns to the Product Backlog"; organisational standard as minimum; the Retrospective inspects the Definition of Done.
- Scrum Guides 2011–2017: DoD expected to expand to "more stringent criteria" as teams mature (removed in 2020). The 2010 guide's "undone work" is the only edition with that term.
- Ward Cunningham, "The WyCash Portfolio Management System" (experience report, 1992): "A little debt speeds development so long as it is paid back promptly with a rewrite… The danger occurs when the debt is not repaid." The paper says "debt", not "technical debt". Cunningham, 2009 video: "I'm never in favor of writing code poorly".
- Martin Fowler, "TechnicalDebtQuadrant" (2009), four quotes from its diagram; "Technical Debt" (2019): costs can't be measured objectively.
- Stripe / Harris Poll, "The Developer Coefficient" (2018): survey estimate of 13.5 hours of a 41.1-hour week on technical debt (the "about a third" is our arithmetic).
- McKinsey (2020): 50 CIOs estimated tech debt at "20 to 40 percent of the value of their entire technology estate before depreciation".
- GIGW 3.0 requires WCAG 2.1 Level AA; using it as a DoD item is our illustration.
- The simulation rates in `model.ts` are made up; the dishes figures are illustrative.
