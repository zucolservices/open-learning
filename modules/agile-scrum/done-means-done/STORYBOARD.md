# Done means done: storyboard

1. **Clean as you go** (analogy): two cooks over a week; one leaves the dishes, the pile grows and dinner gets slower; the other washes up while cooking and stays steady.
2. **Guess first** (predict): Team Shortcut ("code written" = done) delivers 40 points in Sprint 1, Team Thorough 30. Guess Shortcut's Sprint 12 delivery (model: about 16).
3. **Twelve Sprints** ⭐ (simulation): tick DoD items (review, tests, integration, acceptance criteria, security/accessibility); each costs effort now, each skipped leaves undone work that drags on capacity. Chart against "code written only" and full DoD; totals and undone work left; optional fix at the Sprint 6 Retrospective shows slow repayment. Toy model (`model.ts`), labelled as such.
4. **Not all debt is reckless** (explore): Cunningham's 1992 debt metaphor; Fowler's Technical Debt Quadrant with his four example quotes.
5. **Honest Done or hidden debt?** (sort): hardening Sprints, splitting to a truly Done slice, "Done + bug ticket", tightening the DoD at a Retrospective, visible prudent debt.
6. **What to remember**: Scrum Guide DoD quotes (CC BY-SA), Stripe 2018 and McKinsey 2020 survey figures, GIGW 3.0 / WCAG 2.1 AA as an organisational minimum, DoD revisited at Retrospectives.
