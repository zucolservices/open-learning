- The Scrum Guide (November 2020), © Ken Schwaber and Jeff Sutherland, CC BY-SA 4.0: Sprint Review and Retrospective sections verbatim (purposes; "a working session… avoid limiting it to a presentation"; "attendees collaborate on what to do next. The Product Backlog may also be adjusted…"; timeboxes; "second to last event"; "concludes the Sprint"; "The most impactful improvements are addressed as soon as possible. They may even be added to the Sprint Backlog for the next Sprint."; "Assumptions that led them astray…"); DoD: undone work "cannot be released or even presented at the Sprint Review"; "should never be considered a gate to releasing value". 2017 guide: Sprint Backlog "includes at least one high priority process improvement" (softened in 2020).

- Amy Edmondson, "Psychological Safety and Learning Behavior in Work Teams", Administrative Science Quarterly 44(2), 1999: the definition quoted.
- Stefan Wolpers (Scrum.org) anti-pattern lists: "Death by PowerPoint", "Undone is the new 'done'", "Line managers present", "Stakeholder alert", "Groundhog day".
