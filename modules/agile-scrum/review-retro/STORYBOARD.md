# Review & Retrospective: storyboard

1. **Two kinds of looking back** (analogy): after a family wedding, everyone looks at the photos together (Review); the organisers then meet alone about how they worked (Retrospective). Verbatim purposes, timeboxes and who attends.
2. **End of Sprint 4** ⭐ (branching scenario, `scenario.ts`): Review: slides vs a working session vs a sign-off gate; an undone SMS feature (demo it / say openly it isn't Done / hide it); an official's new request (developer promises / PO adds it to the Product Backlog / refuse). Retrospective: who attends (managers and officials / the Scrum Team with the Prime Directive / skip it); a failed release (blame / look at the system / move on); actions (nine / one concrete change / "communicate better"). Each shows a consequence; Wolpers' anti-pattern names where they fit.
3. **What the team learned**: what each choice produced.
4. **Guide or myth?** (sort): sign-off gate, undone work, PO in the retro, skipping retros, retro items in the next Sprint Backlog (2017 vs 2020).
5. **What to remember**: Derby & Larsen's five phases, 4Ls, Speed Boat, Retromat; psychological safety.
