# Splitting stories: storyboard

1. **Slice the cake** (analogy): a three-layer cake (screens, logic, data) sliced by layer ("just the icing") vs top to bottom ("a real slice"); Wake, Lawrence and Adzic quotes.
2. **Split the big feature** ⭐ (sandbox, `model.ts`): a 45-point certificate feature; eight patterns (Workflow steps, Paths, Data, Rules, Interfaces, Operations, Defer performance, Spike) plus a "by layer" trap; each peels off thin stories with sizes; walking-skeleton badge; the remainder until every piece fits a Sprint (≤ 8 points here).
3. **Good split or bad split?** (sort): UPI first, by-team layers, income certificates first, tasks per developer, manual eligibility checks.
4. **Why small?** (choice): Reinertsen's batch-size principles; Humanizing Work's "6 to 10 into a sprint" rule of thumb.
5. **What to remember**: walking skeleton.
