# Sources (fact-checked 2026-09-28, before building)

Full notes: scratchpad `agile/m22-facts.md`.

- Scrum Guide 2020 (quoted exactly): "No changes are made that would endanger the Sprint Goal"; "Quality does not decrease"; "Scope may be clarified and renegotiated with the Product Owner as more is learned"; cancellation only by the Product Owner when the goal "becomes obsolete"; "never be considered a gate to releasing value"; work not meeting the DoD "cannot be released or even presented at the Sprint Review"; Developers "internally decide who does what, when, and how".
- Agile Manifesto principle 8: "sponsors, developers, and users should be able to maintain a constant pace indefinitely."
- Mary Iqbal, Scrum.org blog (2022): Developers and PO decide together on urgent work; teams that get interrupted leave capacity free.
- John Pencavel, "The Productivity of Working Hours", IZA DP 8129 (2014) / Economic Journal (2015): output proportional to hours below about 49 a week; "Output at 70 hours differs little from output at 56 hours".
- WHO/ILO news release (17 May 2021): 55+ hours a week linked to a 35% higher risk of stroke and 17% higher risk of dying from ischaemic heart disease, versus 35–40 hours.
- DoPT O.M. F.No.12/2/2023-JCA (3 July 2025), central government holidays 2026: Dussehra Tuesday 20 October 2026.
- Team, client, points and rates are illustrative.
