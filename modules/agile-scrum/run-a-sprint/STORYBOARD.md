# Capstone: run a sprint: storyboard

1. **The briefing**: a Bengaluru team, a clinic chain's appointment app, Sprint Goal "Patients can reschedule an appointment online". Calendar for 12–23 October 2026 with Dussehra (Tue 20) struck out: nine working days. Velocity history 24/22/26, forecast ≈ 21.6.
2. **Run the Sprint** ⭐ (simulation + branching): day by day with a live burndown (work left, ideal, Sprint Backlog size, Dussehra band). Calls: Sprint Planning (27 / 19 / 13 points), a side request, a three-day sick absence, a production payment bug after the holiday, the Sprint Review and a Retrospective improvement. Each choice shows how it tends to play out; restart any time. Model in `model.ts` (overloaded Sprints spread effort, so goal items finish late; skipped tests mean the goal isn't Done).
3. **The debrief**: Sprint Goal met or not, points Done, work back to the Product Backlog, new debt, weekend work, the bug's wait; each call with a stronger option where one exists.
4. **One more surprise** (choice): the goal becomes obsolete; only the Product Owner may cancel.
5. **What to remember**: plan to the pace, protect the goal, never trade quality, ship when Done; Pencavel and WHO/ILO on long hours; the Manifesto's sustainable pace.
