# Sources (fact-checked 2026-09-28, before building)

Full notes: scratchpad `agile/m19-facts.md`.

- Scrum Guide 2020: teams "typically 10 or fewer people"; split into "multiple cohesive Scrum Teams, each focused on the same product" sharing one Product Goal, Product Backlog and Product Owner.
- Fred Brooks, _The Mythical Man-Month_ (1975): "Adding manpower to a late software project makes it later"; n(n−1)/2. Melvin Conway, Datamation (April 1968).
- Nexus Guide (January 2021): "approximately three to nine Scrum Teams"; Nexus Integration Team; "A Nexus Sprint Review replaces individual Scrum Team Sprint Reviews".
- LeSS Rules (November 2024): one PO, Product Backlog, DoD and Sprint; Sprint Planning One with the PO and teams; Overall Retrospective; Basic LeSS 2–"8" teams; LeSS Huge with Requirement Areas of 4–8 teams and Area POs; "results in an integrated whole product".
- SAFe (framework.scaledagile.com, core 6.0, March 2023): ARTs "generally made up of 50 -125 people"; PI "a timebox of 8-12 weeks"; RTE, Product Management, System Architect, Business Owners; ART Sync, System Demo, Inspect & Adapt; four configurations. AI-Native SAFe press release, 23 June 2026.
- Scrum@Scale Guide v2.1 (February 2022): Scrum of Scrums of "4 or 5" teams; Executive Action Team ("how") and Executive MetaScrum ("what"); "minimum viable bureaucracy"; "a fully integrated set of potentially shippable increments of product at the end of every Sprint"; "If an organization cannot Scrum, it cannot scale."
- Digital.ai, 18th State of Agile (October 2025, about 350 respondents): SAFe most reported; a combined 74% hybrid or home-grown.
- Ken Schwaber, "unSAFe at any speed" (6 August 2013). Jeremiah Lee, "Spotify's Failed #SquadGoals" (19 April 2020), quoting an agile coach.
- Skelton & Pais, _Team Topologies_ (2019): stream-aligned, enabling, complicated-subsystem and platform teams.
- Wedding analogy, teams, features and team counts in the explorer are illustrative.
