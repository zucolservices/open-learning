# Kanban & WIP limits: storyboard

1. **Rush hour** (analogy): a jammed road vs ramp-metered flow (Kanban University's motorway picture).
2. **Kanban in three practices** (step-through): the Kanban Guide (May 2025) definition, three practices, Definition of Workflow, explicit WIP control, the four flow metrics verbatim.
3. **Run the board** ⭐ (simulation, `model.ts`): To do → Develop (3 people) → Test (1 tester, bottleneck) → Done over 60 days, ~1.1 arrivals/day, switching penalty; WIP limits for Develop and Test; day slider and play; throughput, average WIP, cycle time, oldest item; no limits (≈30 WIP, 0.23/day), 5/2 (0.75/day, 8.5 days), 1/1 (starved).
4. **Little's Law** (predict): 6 WIP ÷ 2/day = 3 days; averages over a finished period, not single-item forecasts (Little 2011; Vacanti).
5. **Kanban or myth?** (sort); Toyota 1954 supermarket method; Anderson 2004–2010.
