# Sources (fact-checked 2026-09-28, before building)

Full notes: scratchpad `agile/m13-facts.md`.

- The Kanban Guide (kanbanguides.org, v2025.5, May 2025): "Kanban is a strategy for optimizing the flow of value through a process."; three practices; Definition of Workflow; "Kanban system members must explicitly control the number of work items in a workflow from started to finished."; the four flow metrics (renamed from "Kanban Measures"). The term "WIP limit" is not in the 2025 guide; presented as the usual way to control WIP.
- John D.C. Little (1961) and "Little's Law as Viewed on Its 50th Anniversary" (2011): "we are in the measurement business, not the forecasting business". Daniel Vacanti, "Little's Law for Professional Scrum with Kanban" (Scrum.org, 2018): cycle time = WIP ÷ throughput; "was never designed for—making deterministic forecasts".
- Toyota 75-year history: "supermarket method" introduced 1954, evolving into kanban. David J. Anderson: Microsoft XIT (2004), Corbis (2006–07), Kanban (Blue Hole Press, 2010).
- Kanban University's guide: motorway metaphor ("fully utilized… very little is moving").
- "Stop starting, start finishing": no known author.
- Reinertsen Q3 (utilisation and queues) informs the "busy isn't faster" point.
- The board simulation is illustrative (see `model.ts`).
