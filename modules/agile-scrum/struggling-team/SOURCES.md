# Sources (fact-checked 2026-09-28, before building)

Full notes: scratchpad `agile/m23-facts.md` (builds on m13 and m14).

- "Stop starting, start finishing": a well-known Kanban slogan; no reliable originator found (Kanban University calls it "a good mantra").
- Kanban Guide (2025): Work Item Age is "The elapsed time between when a work item started and the current date."
- Toyota (2006): "Ask 'why' five times about every matter"; made famous by Taiichi Ohno.
- Norm Kerth, _Project Retrospectives_ (2001): the Prime Directive, quoted in its common modern form.
- Julia Rozovsky, re:Work (17 November 2015): psychological safety "far and away the most important of the five dynamics". Amy Edmondson (1999) definition.
- Eliyahu Goldratt (with Jeff Cox), _The Goal_ (1984); five focusing steps (identify, exploit, subordinate, elevate, repeat without inertia).
- Scrum Guide 2020: Scrum Teams are cross-functional; the Scrum Master coaches "self-management and cross-functionality"; "Those wanting to change the Product Backlog can do so by trying to convince the Product Owner."
- Flow data: `../kanban-wip/model` with optional `testers`, `size` and `bursts` settings added for this module (defaults unchanged). Team, quotes and notes are illustrative.
