# Capstone: the struggling team: storyboard

1. **The case**: Team Falcon (an insurance claims app) "does Scrum" but delivers slowly. Three voices: the client, the manager (more people or a better tool), a developer juggling about fifteen things. Examine, diagnose, then treat.
2. **Gather the evidence** ⭐ (investigate): four tabs from module 13's board model with no WIP limits: the board today (48 items in Develop, Test empty, oldest 46 days), the cumulative flow diagram, the cycle-time scatterplot, and retro notes. Pin clues into a notebook that explains each one.
3. **What's really wrong?** (sort): too much WIP, big stories and side requests are supported; "the tester is slow", "people aren't working hard" and "a better tool" aren't. Five Whys as the habit.
4. **Choose the first changes** ⭐ (simulation): pick up to three of seven changes (WIP limits, swarming on testing, splitting stories, routing requests via the PO, Prime Directive retros, overtime, a new tool). Before/after cycle time, items finished, WIP and oldest item, with the after-CFD. Swarming alone does nothing until WIP is limited (Theory of Constraints).
5. **What to remember**: the Retrospective Prime Directive, "stop starting, start finishing", Project Aristotle, and the end of the track.
