# Agile anti-patterns: storyboard

1. **Teaching to the test** (analogy + toggle): a school judged on pass rates; velocity as a planning aid vs a +10% target. Velocity climbs, features users get stay flat. Goodhart (1975) and Strathern's 1997 wording.
2. **The symptom clinic** ⭐ (fix the problem): six teams' symptoms (velocity target, Water-Scrum-Fall, Zombie Scrum, status-report Daily Scrum, hardening Sprints, mini-waterfall). Name the pattern (feedback with the source), then choose a first fix (likely / unlikely to help, with reasons). Revisit freely.
3. **Healthy or anti-pattern?** (sort): Sprint Backlog changing, forecast treated as a promise, ranking teams by velocity, skipping retros, reordering after the Review.
4. **What to remember**: ScrumBut, Fowler's Flaccid Scrum (plainly named), Dark Scrum, Zombie Scrum; the Guide's line on changing Scrum; Jeffries' story-points apology.
