# Inspect & adapt: storyboard

1. **Learning to drive** (analogy): point the car and look away vs small constant corrections on a winding road; Kent Beck's driving lesson (XP Explained, 1999).
2. **Three pillars** (step-through): transparency, inspection, adaptation with the Scrum Guide's own definitions and links ("Transparency enables inspection…", "Inspection enables adaptation…"); empiricism.
3. **Steer to a moving target** ⭐ (simulation, `model.ts`): what users need drifts over 26 weeks; inspect every 1/2/4/13/26 weeks; transparency on/off (stale status reports). Average gap vs time spent inspecting. Lessons: shorter loops fit better but aren't free; without transparency, frequent inspection doesn't help.
4. **Defined or empirical?** (sort): Schwaber's 1995 DuPont story (Ogunnaike); bread, payroll, flat-pack (defined) vs citizen app, chatbot (empirical).
5. **When does Scrum inspect?** (choice): every event, and whenever something new is learned.
6. **What to remember**: Shewhart/Deming PDSA as an echo, not the origin.
