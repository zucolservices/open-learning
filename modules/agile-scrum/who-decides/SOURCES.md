# Sources (fact-checked 2026-09-28, before building)

Full notes and the 26-row decision table: scratchpad `agile/m05-facts.md`.

- The Scrum Guide (November 2020), © Ken Schwaber and Jeff Sutherland, CC BY-SA 4.0. Verbatim: Product Owner accountabilities and "Those wanting to change the Product Backlog can do so by trying to convince the Product Owner."; Developers' accountabilities; Scrum Master service lists and "Causing the removal of impediments…"; "no sub-teams or hierarchies"; "self-managing, meaning they internally decide who does what, when, and how."; Sprint Goal ("The whole Scrum Team then collaborates to define a Sprint Goal"); "How this is done is at the sole discretion of the Developers…"; sizing ("The Developers who will be doing the work are responsible for the sizing…"); "Only the Product Owner has the authority to cancel the Sprint."; "Quality does not decrease"; organisational DoD "as a minimum"; the Developers choose the Daily Scrum's structure.
- Wording traps avoided: the 2020 guide never says "manager", "assign", "task", "estimate" or "servant-leader" (2017 only).
- Robert K. Greenleaf, "The Servant as Leader" (1970), per the Greenleaf Center.
- "What goes wrong" consequences are illustrative, from common practice, not the guide.
