# Who decides what?: storyboard

1. **Three accountabilities** (analogy): a restaurant kitchen (owner sets the menu, cooks decide how, someone keeps the kitchen running); verbatim accountability lists for Product Owner, Developers and Scrum Master; no hierarchies; self-managing.
2. **Who decides?** ⭐ (branching scenario, `situations.ts`): nine situations on a citizen-portal project (urgent stakeholder request mid-Sprint, Sprint Goal, how to build, sizing, cancelling a Sprint, a blocker, skipping testing, the organisation's minimum DoD, the Daily Scrum's format). Six possible answers; each shows the Scrum Guide sentence that settles it and, for a wrong answer, what tends to go wrong.
3. **Who decides what, at a glance** (summary by accountability); budgets, contracts and staffing sit with client and company governance.
4. **Is the Scrum Master the boss?** (choice): project managers renamed Scrum Masters who assign tasks.
5. **What to remember**.
