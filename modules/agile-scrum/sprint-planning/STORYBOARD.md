# The Sprint & Sprint Planning: storyboard

1. **Why, what and how** (step-through): a weekend trek (agree why, pack what fits, plan the route; change the route, not the goal); the Scrum Guide's three Sprint Planning topics, verbatim; the Sprint Goal is finalised in Planning; the timebox.
2. **Plan a Sprint** ⭐ (build, `model.ts`): five people, ten days; Gandhi Jayanti, a three-day leave and a support rotation leave 37 person-days; forecast 17 points from recent actuals (21, 24, 18 at ~45 person-days) vs a wishful 24. Choose one of three proposed goals, pick items (goal-critical ones flagged), then run the Sprint: goal items first, one grows by 2; outcome shows done/not done and notes on off-goal items and over/under-planning.
3. **What does the team commit to?** (choice): the Sprint Goal; items are a forecast (2010 → 2011 → 2020 history).
4. **Bigger than we thought** (choice): renegotiate scope with the Product Owner, keep the goal and quality.
5. **What to remember**: story points and velocity as optional practices (Jeffries' "I'm sorry now"); "yesterday's weather".
