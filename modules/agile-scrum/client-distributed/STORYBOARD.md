# Client-facing & distributed teams: storyboard

1. **A suit made far away** (analogy, step-through): a Bengaluru tailor and a London customer; measurements once (wrong suit), fittings through a cousin (every question waits), a weekly video fitting (fits). NASSCOM FY2026 context.
2. **Guess the overlap** (predict): Bengaluru 9–6 and New York 9–6 in winter share no hours.
3. **Two clocks** (explore): client city (London / New York) × season; IST bars, overlap strip; slide the Bengaluru start time to buy overlap, with the cost made visible. Fowler's "give and take".
4. **Five moments** ⭐ (branching scenario): who decides (proxy PO), the Daily Scrum, a side request, the Sprint Review, saying "not yet". Each choice shows how it's likely to go; learners can revisit.
5. **What to remember**: keep the decider close, guard the overlap, route requests to the PO, make "not yet" normal; Herbsleb & Mockus 2003, Bird et al. 2009, Fowler's ambassadors and "polite acceptance".
