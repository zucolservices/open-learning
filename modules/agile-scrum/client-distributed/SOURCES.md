# Sources (fact-checked 2026-09-28, before building)

Full notes: scratchpad `agile/m18-facts.md`.

- Scrum Guide 2020: PO is "one person, not a committee"; may delegate but "remains accountable"; others change the backlog "by trying to convince the Product Owner"; the Sprint Review "should never be considered a gate to releasing value"; "Only the Product Owner has the authority to cancel the Sprint." No co-location requirement.
- "Proxy Product Owner" is not a Scrum Guide term. Scrum.org blog (2025): "typically becomes a bottleneck"; Roman Pichler (2010): "an attempt to superficially treat a systemic issue".
- Time zones: IST is UTC+5:30 with no daylight saving; UK BST/GMT and US EDT/EST rules. Bengaluru 9–18 overlaps London 4½ h (summer) / 3½ h (winter) and New York not at all.
- Martin Fowler, "Using an Agile Software Process with Offshore Development" (2003, updated 2006): ambassadors ("The plane fares soon repay themselves"), "give and take in picking the time for calls", "polite acceptance is often a sign of an important issue not getting discussed".
- Herbsleb & Mockus, IEEE TSE 29(6), 2003 (Lucent): distributed work took "about two and one-half times as long". Bird et al., 2009 (Windows Vista): "negligible difference in failures".
- NASSCOM Strategic Review, February 2026: tech industry about US$315 billion for FY2026 (estimate), mostly exports.
- The team, client, names and five moments are illustrative.
