# Scrum on one page: storyboard

1. **A relay, or a rugby team?** (analogy): Takeuchi & Nonaka (HBR, 1986) contrasted sequential "relay" development with a "rugby" team moving together; Sutherland (1993) and the 1995 public presentation.
2. **Scrum on one page** ⭐ (animated infographic, `scrum-map.tsx`, shared with the track showcase): Scrum Team (PO, SM, Developers); Product Backlog → Planning → Sprint Backlog → Sprint (Daily Scrum inside) → Increment → Review → Retrospective → next Sprint. Click any part for its verbatim purpose, when it happens and its timebox/commitment; "Follow one Sprint" tours them in order.
3. **In the guide, or added on?** (sort): Sprint Goal, DoD, Retrospective vs story points/velocity, user stories, two-week Sprints.
4. **How long is Sprint Planning?** (choice): a timebox is a maximum (8h for a one-month Sprint, usually shorter), not a formula.
5. **What to remember**: 3 accountabilities, 5 events, 3 artifacts; the five values; "the result is not Scrum".
