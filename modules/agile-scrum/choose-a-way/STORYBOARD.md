# Scrum, Kanban or both?: storyboard

1. **A banquet or a café?** (analogy): a banquet kitchen plans in batches to set times (like Sprints); a café serves orders as they come with a limit on how many are in progress (like Kanban).
2. **Four teams, four choices** ⭐ (branching scenario): a citizen-portal product team, an L2 support team, a fixed-bid project team and a platform team. For each, pick Scrum, Kanban or Scrum with Kanban practices; see a fit verdict, why, and how the other options would fare. A summary appears once all four are chosen.
3. **Scrum, Kanban and myths** (sort): Kanban has no planning, Scrum can't use WIP limits, Kanban is only for maintenance, teams can change their way of working. Scrumban introduced as a family of hybrids.
4. **What to remember**: match the work, combine freely, Kanban has cadences, start where you are (Kanban University change principles).
