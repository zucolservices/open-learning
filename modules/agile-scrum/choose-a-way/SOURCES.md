# Sources (fact-checked 2026-09-28, before building)

Full notes: scratchpad `agile/m15-facts.md`.

- Scrum.org, _The Kanban Guide for Scrum Teams_ (Jan 2021): "does not replace or discount any part of The Scrum Guide"; adds visualisation of the workflow, limiting WIP, active management of work items and inspecting the Definition of Workflow; no additional events.
- Corey Ladas, "Scrum-ban" essay (2008) and _Scrumban_ (Modus Cooperandi Press, 2009). Presented as a family of hybrids, not an official framework.
- Kanban University: change management principles ("Start with what you do now", "Agree to pursue improvement through evolutionary change", "Encourage acts of leadership at all levels"); "never a question of using Kanban versus a given methodology".
- David J. Anderson (2015): seven Kanban cadences, including Replenishment and Delivery Planning (frequencies are examples).
- _The Kanban Guide_ (2025): "not limited to any specific industry or context".
- Atlassian: Kanban suits teams with "lots of incoming requests that vary in priority and size".
- Jeff Sutherland, "Money for Nothing, Change for Free" (2008); Larman & Vodde, _Agile Contracts Primer_: fixed-price, fixed-scope is usually lose-lose but "Scrum can be used with a fixed-content Release Backlog".
- The four teams and their outcomes are illustrative.
