# Storyboard: Types of fact table

1. **Three ways to keep score** (story): statement lines, month-end balances, loan tracker.
2. **One process, three fact tables** ⭐ (simulation): a day slider over three orders; transaction, periodic and accumulating views.
3. **What can you add up?** (explore): additive sales, semi-additive balances, non-additive margins.
4. **Facts with no numbers** (explore): attendance; coverage minus activity.
5. **Which kind of fact table?** (checkpoint `which-fact-table`).
6. **What to remember** (wrap).
