# Sources: Evaluating agents (fact-checked 2026-10-04)

- C. E. Jimenez et al., "SWE-bench", ICLR 2024 (2,294 tasks, 12 repos); OpenAI, "Introducing SWE-bench Verified" (13 Aug 2024) and its Feb 2026 decision to stop reporting it.
- S. Yao et al., "τ-bench" (Sierra, Jun 2024): pass^k, "the chance that all k i.i.d. task trials are successful"; τ²-bench (9 Jun 2025).
- GAIA (Nov 2023), WebArena (Jul 2023), OSWorld (Apr 2024), Terminal-Bench (2025).
- METR, "Measuring AI Ability to Complete Long Tasks" (19 Mar 2025) and Time Horizon 1.1 update (29 Jan 2026).
- Anthropic engineering, "Demystifying evals for AI agents" (9 Jan 2026): 20–50 tasks, grade outcomes, mixed graders, read transcripts; worked example (75% → ~42% for 3 in a row).
- Google ADK evaluation (tool_trajectory_avg_score modes); LangChain agentevals (trajectory match modes).

The refund agents and runs are illustrative.
