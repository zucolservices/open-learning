Full notes: scratchpad `obs/m18-facts.md` (raw pages in `obs/m17/`); regulatory timing from `obs/m21-facts.md`.

- Google, _Site Reliability Engineering_, ch. 14 "Managing Incidents": roles of incident command, operational work, communication and planning; "a clear separation of responsibilities allows individuals more autonomy than they might otherwise have, since they need not second-guess their colleagues"; "the commander holds all positions that they have not delegated"; "The operations team should be the only group modifying the system during an incident"; "It is better to declare an incident early…"; three criteria for declaring.
- PagerDuty Incident Response documentation: roles (Incident Commander, Deputy, Scribe, Subject Matter Expert, Customer Liaison, Internal Liaison); SEV-1 to SEV-5 definitions (quoted); "If you are unsure which level an incident is … treat it as the higher one."
- FIRESCOPE / Cal OES, "ICS History and Progression": the 1970 Southern California fire season, 13 days, 16 deaths; ICS developed in the 1970s.
- Atlassian incident communication guidance: "never going more than one hour without sending an update"; always say when the next update is due.
- CERT-In directions (28 Apr 2022): report listed cyber security incidents within 6 hours. RBI Master Directions for non-bank Payment System Operators (30 Jul 2024): report unusual incidents including outages of critical systems within 6 hours of detection.
