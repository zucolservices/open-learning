# Capstone: observing a payments platform (Observability, module 21)

1. **The brief** (story): an illustrative UPI platform; five needs; NPCI's business vs technical declines.
2. **Make the choices** ⭐ (build): nine decisions (instrumentation, SLI, labels, latency, logs, tracing, paging, watching the watchers, incident process), each with a note.
3. **The bad month** ⭐ (branching): nine incidents (slow bank, salary day, slower after a release, 3 a.m. blip, silent failure, monitoring down too, log review, regulator's clock, contract renewal); holds / degrades / breaks. No score.
4. **What to fix first** (sort checkpoint `obs-fix-first`).
5. **The whole track** (wrap).
