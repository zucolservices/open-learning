# Storyboard: Observability platforms and cost

1. **Same calls, different bills** (story): phone plans priced differently; one illustrative month of telemetry; Grafana 2026 survey stats.
2. **Cut the bill** ⭐ (simulation): toggle five levers (one makes you blind) and watch four platforms' list-price bills move.
3. **What you pay for** (explore): pricing units across eight platforms, dated.
4. **Build or buy** (compare): self-hosted vs SaaS; OpenTelemetry keeps options open.
5. **Saving or going blind?** (checkpoint `safe-or-blind`).
6. **What to remember** (wrap).
