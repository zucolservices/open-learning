# Sources (fact-checked 2026-09-29, before building)

Full notes: scratchpad `rag/m01-facts.md`.

- Lewis et al., "Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Knowledge-Intensive NLP Tasks" (arXiv 22 May 2020; NeurIPS 2020; Facebook AI Research, UCL, NYU): "models which combine pre-trained parametric and non-parametric memory"; "the parametric memory is a pre-trained seq2seq model and the non-parametric memory is a dense vector index of Wikipedia"; contrasts with closed-book QA; index-swap result (68–70% vs 4–12% on changed world leaders). The paper claims provenance, not user-facing citations.
- Ovadia et al., "Fine-Tuning or Retrieval?" (EMNLP 2024): RAG "consistently outperforms" unsupervised fine-tuning for knowledge injection.
- Gekhman et al., "Does Fine-Tuning LLMs on New Knowledge Encourage Hallucinations?" (EMNLP 2024): new facts "learned significantly slower" and "linearly increase the model's tendency to hallucinate".
- Context windows of about one million tokens in current vendor docs (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google), September 2026; no model versions named.
- Liu et al., "Lost in the Middle" (TACL 2024). Li et al., "Retrieval Augmented Generation or Long-Context LLMs?" (EMNLP 2024 Industry): long context "consistently outperforms RAG" on average when resourced; "RAG's significantly lower cost remains a distinct advantage".
- Moffatt v. Air Canada, 2024 BCCRT 149 (14 February 2024): C$812.02 in total; "It makes no difference whether the information comes from a static page or a chatbot." The decision doesn't describe how the chatbot worked.
- Microsoft Source Asia (23 May 2023) on Jugalbandi: built with AI4Bharat, OpenNyAI and others; "retrieves information on relevant programs – usually written in English – and relays it back in local language"; 10 languages and 171 programmes at launch.
- The leave policy, its numbers and the question are made up.
