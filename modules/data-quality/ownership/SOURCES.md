# Sources: Ownership and stewardship (fact-checked 2026-10-04)

- DAMA International, DAMA-DMBOK, 2nd ed. (2017): data governance definition; data owner as a business data steward with approval authority.
- Z. Dehghani, "How to Move Beyond a Monolithic Data Lake to a Distributed Data Mesh" (martinfowler.com, May 2019) and "Data Mesh Principles and Logical Architecture" (Dec 2020).
- RACI: general responsibility-assignment matrix (project management practice).
- Docs: Databricks Unity Catalog object ownership; DataHub ownership types (Technical Owner, Business Owner, Data Steward); OpenMetadata ownership (teams recommended); dbt groups (required owner).

The company, datasets and incidents are made up; the RACI chart is a teaching example.
