# Sources: Data contracts (fact-checked 2026-10-04)

- A. Jones, "Improving Data Quality with Data Contracts", GoCardless, 23 Dec 2021; A. Jones, Driving Data Quality with Data Contracts, Packt, 2023.
- C. Sanderson, "The Rise of Data Contracts", 22 Aug 2022 (Convoy; "non-consensual API").
- Bitol: Open Data Contract Standard releases (v3.0.0 Oct 2024, v3.1.0 Dec 2025, v3.2.0 Sep 2026), full example contract (v3.2.0 field names), graduation from LF AI & Data (July 2026); PayPal template repository pointing to ODCS.
- datacontract.com / datacontract-cli (Data Contract Specification deprecated in favour of ODCS).
- dbt docs: model contracts (dbt 1.5, 27 Apr 2023; columns and types; not Python or ephemeral models).

The orders feed and its changes are made up; the contract is shortened.
