# Storyboard: Finding personal data in your systems

1. **Packing to move** (story): the loft, the friend's garage, the old flat; a data map defined.
2. **Where does it hide?** ⭐ (fix the problem): inspect eight systems of a made-up grocery app; each reveals hidden personal data and adds a row to the data map.
3. **Can a scanner find it for you?** (explore): Macie, Sensitive Data Protection, Purview and Presidio against six Indian identifiers.
4. **Aadhaar in a support ticket** (explore): store, mask or hash; why hashing isn't enough; mask logs rather than delete them.
5. **Does it belong on the map?** (checkpoint `dpdp-map`).
6. **What to remember** (wrap).
