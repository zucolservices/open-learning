# Storyboard: Withdrawal and consent managers

1. **Joining is one tap. Leaving?** (story): the gym that's easy to join and hard to leave.
2. **Follow the stop signal** ⭐ (step-through): a marketing-SMS withdrawal travels through seven systems; what stops (incl. the SMS processor) and what continues (paid order, tax invoice).
3. **One place to manage every yes** (explore): consent managers' eligibility and duties; dates; Account Aggregator analogy.
4. **Is the way out as easy as the way in?** (explore): four withdrawal designs judged for comparable ease.
5. **Stop or carry on?** (checkpoint `dpdp-withdraw`).
6. **What to remember** (wrap).
