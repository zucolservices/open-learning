# Sources: Access, correction and erasure (fact-checked 2026-10-08)

- DPDP Act 2023, s.11(1) (summary of personal data and processing; identities of all fiduciaries and processors shared with, and description of data shared), s.11(2) (exception for law-authorised requests), s.12 (correction, completion, updating, erasure; s.12(3) refusal where necessary for the specified purpose or for compliance with law), s.11–12 cover data processed on consent incl. s.7(a), s.13 (grievance), s.14 (nomination), s.2(j) (parents, guardians), s.8(7)(b) (cause processors to erase), s.15 and Schedule item 5 (up to ₹10,000).
- DPDP Rules 2025, Rule 14(1) (publish the means and identifier), 14(3) (grievance response period at most 90 days), 14(5) (identifier). No fixed deadline for access, correction or erasure; no fee. Rule 8(3) (logs at least one year). In force from May 2027.
- No portability, objection, restriction or automated-decision right in the Act. Backups are not addressed; suppress-and-rotate is presented as common practice, not law.
