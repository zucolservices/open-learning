# Sources: The Data Protection Board and penalties (fact-checked 2026-10-08)

- DPDP Act 2023, ss.18–26 (Board), s.27 (functions), s.28 (digital office, civil-court powers, interim orders), s.29 (appeal to TDSAT within 60 days; six-month target), s.31 (mediation), s.32 (voluntary undertakings), s.33(1) (penalty only for a significant breach after inquiry and hearing), s.33(2)(a)–(g) (factors), s.34 (Consolidated Fund of India), s.37 (blocking: Board reference after penalties in two or more instances; Central Government decides after a hearing), s.39; Schedule items 1–7 (₹250 cr, ₹200 cr, ₹200 cr, ₹150 cr, ₹10,000, undertaking breach up to the original cap, ₹50 cr). No compensation for individuals and no criminal offences in the Act.
- G.S.R. 844(E) and 845(E), 13 Nov 2025 (Board; Chairperson plus four Members; NCR). DPDP Rules 2025, Rules 17–22. ss.27–34 and s.37 from May 2027 (G.S.R. 843(E)).
