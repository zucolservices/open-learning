# Sources: Why a data protection law (fact-checked 2026-10-08)

- _Justice K.S. Puttaswamy (Retd.) v. Union of India_, W.P. (C) 494 of 2012, judgment of 24 Aug 2017 (nine judges, unanimous): privacy protected under Article 21 and Part III.
- Committee of Experts under Justice B.N. Srikrishna (constituted 31 Jul 2017; report and draft bill 27 Jul 2018). PRS Legislative Research briefs on the PDP Bill 2019 (introduced 11 Dec 2019; JPC report 16 Dec 2021) and the DPDP Bill 2023 (Lok Sabha 7 Aug, Rajya Sabha 9 Aug 2023).
- Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (No. 22 of 2023), Gazette of India, 11 Aug 2023: s.44(2)(a) omits IT Act s.43A.
- Gazette notifications dated 13 Nov 2025: G.S.R. 843(E) (commencement: Board provisions immediately; s.6(9) and s.27(1)(d) after one year; ss.3–17, 27–34, 36, 37 and s.44(2) after 18 months), G.S.R. 844(E) and 845(E) (Board established; four Members; NCR), G.S.R. 846(E) (DPDP Rules 2025; Rule 1 commencement). PIB backgrounder, 17 Nov 2025.
- MeitY circular F. No. 2(1)/2026-Pers.I, 6 May 2026 (applications for Board Chairperson and Members); LiveLaw, 1 Aug 2026 (no appointments yet). MeitY consultation of 23 Jan 2026 on a shorter runway (reported by Mondaq, Apr 2026); no amending notification found as of 8 Oct 2026.
- IT (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011.
- Incidents: Air India statement on the SITA PSS attack (21 May 2021); Rajya Sabha reply on the AIIMS ransomware attack (Dec 2022); Star Health Insurance statement and Reuters reporting (Sep 2024). All predate the Act's duties.
