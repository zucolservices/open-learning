# Sources: Security safeguards (fact-checked 2026-10-08)

- DPDP Act 2023, s.8(3) (completeness, accuracy, consistency where data is likely to be used for a decision affecting the person or disclosed to another fiduciary), s.8(4) (technical and organisational measures), s.8(5) (reasonable security safeguards, incl. processing by processors), s.2(u) (breach includes loss of availability), s.33 and Schedule item 1 (up to ₹250 crore; imposed after inquiry for significant breaches; s.33(2) factors incl. mitigation).
- DPDP Rules 2025, Rule 6(1)(a)–(g) ("at the minimum"; encryption, obfuscation, masking or virtual tokens; access control; logs, monitoring and review; continued processing such as backups; logs and personal data kept for one year; contract terms with processors; technical and organisational measures). No standard or algorithm named. In force from May 2027.
- SPDI Rules 2011, Rule 8 (named IS/ISO/IEC 27001) until s.44(2) takes effect in May 2027. CERT-In Directions of 28 Apr 2022 (six-hour reporting, 180-day logs) apply in parallel.
