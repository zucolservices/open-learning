# Sources: What the Act covers (fact-checked 2026-10-08)

- DPDP Act 2023, s.3(a) (digital personal data in India: collected digitally, or non-digitally and digitised later), s.3(b) (outside India, in connection with offering goods or services to Data Principals in India), s.3(c)(i) (personal or domestic purpose) and s.3(c)(ii) (made publicly available by the Data Principal or by a person under a legal obligation), with the blogging illustration. s.2(t) personal data ("identifiable"), s.2(n), s.2(x).
- The draft DPDP Bill 2022 (cl.4(2)) also covered profiling of Data Principals in India; the 2023 Act dropped it. Treat the profiling scenario as an interpretation.
- s.17(1)(d): processing in India of personal data of Data Principals outside India under a contract with a person outside India; s.8(1) and s.8(5) still apply.
- The words "anonymised" and "sensitive personal data" do not appear in the Act or the Rules.
- G.S.R. 843(E), 13 Nov 2025: s.3 commences 18 months after publication (May 2027).
