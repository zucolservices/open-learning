# Storyboard: What the Act covers

1. **The ledger and the app** (story): a paper ledger (out), the same data in a billing app (in), a shop abroad selling to India (in).
2. **In scope or not?** ⭐ (simulation): ten scenarios; guess, then watch a five-question path through s.2(t) and s.3 light up and stop where the case leaves scope.
3. **Across the border** (explore): offering to India (in), profiling only (probably out), Indian firm for foreign clients (in, mostly exempt under s.17(1)(d)).
4. **Four myths about scope** (explore): public, anonymised, sensitive-only, paper.
5. **Covered or not?** (checkpoint `dpdp-scope`).
6. **What to remember** (wrap).
