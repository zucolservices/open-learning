# Sources: Who's who under the Act (fact-checked 2026-10-08)

- Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (Gazette, 11 Aug 2023): s.2(c) Board, s.2(f) child (under 18), s.2(g) Consent Manager, s.2(i) Data Fiduciary ("alone or in conjunction with other persons determines the purpose and means"), s.2(j) Data Principal (includes parents or lawful guardian of a child, and the lawful guardian of a person with disability), s.2(k) Data Processor, s.2(s) person (includes the State), s.2(y) "she" irrespective of gender, s.2(z) Significant Data Fiduciary (notified under s.10).
- s.8(1): fiduciary responsible for processing by its processor "irrespective of any agreement to the contrary"; s.8(2) processor only under a valid contract; s.8(6) breach intimation by the fiduciary.
- G.S.R. 843(E), 13 Nov 2025: s.2 and the Board provisions in force; s.8 from May 2027.
