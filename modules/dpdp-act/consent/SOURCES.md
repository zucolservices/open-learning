# Sources: Consent that counts (fact-checked 2026-10-08)

- DPDP Act 2023, s.6(1) (free, specific, informed, unconditional, unambiguous, clear affirmative action, limited to necessary data; telemedicine/contact-list illustration), s.6(2) (invalid to the extent of infringement; insurance/waiver illustration), s.6(3) (clear and plain language; English or Eighth Schedule language; DPO or other contact), s.6(10) (fiduciary must prove notice and consent). In force from May 2027 (G.S.R. 843(E)).
- The Act does not mention pre-ticked boxes, bundling or dark patterns; those conclusions follow from s.6(1)'s wording. Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules 2020, Rule 4(9), expressly bars recording consent through pre-ticked checkboxes.
- CCPA Guidelines for Prevention and Regulation of Dark Patterns, 30 Nov 2023 (13 specified patterns). CCPA orders: McAfee (20 May 2026, ₹1 lakh) and PhysicsWallah (1 Jun 2026, ₹5 lakh), PIB release 3 Jun 2026. Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) (Amendment) Rules 2026, notified 9 Sep 2026 (yearly dark-pattern self-audit from 1 Jan 2027).
- s.5/s.6 failures fall under Schedule item 7 (up to ₹50 crore).
