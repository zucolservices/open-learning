# Sources: Data processors and contracts (fact-checked 2026-10-08)

- DPDP Act 2023, s.2(k) (processor acts on behalf of a fiduciary), s.8(1) (fiduciary responsible "irrespective of any agreement to the contrary"), s.8(2) (processor only under a valid contract), s.8(5) (safeguards incl. processing by processors), s.8(6) (breach intimation by the fiduciary), s.8(7)(b) (cause processor to erase), s.6(6) (cause processors to cease), s.11(1)(b) (identities of processors on request). "Valid contract" is not defined; Indian Contract Act 1872 applies. No sub-processor provision (the 2022 draft had one).
- DPDP Rules 2025, Rule 6(1)(f) (contract with processor to take reasonable security safeguards) is the only contract term the Rules name; Rule 7 (fiduciary's 72-hour report runs from its own awareness). In force from May 2027.
- AWS Data Processing Addendum, Google Cloud Data Processing Addendum (last modified 8 Jun 2026) and Microsoft Products and Services DPA (May 2026): generic "applicable law" wording; no DPDP-specific section found (Microsoft unconfirmed).
