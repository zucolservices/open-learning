# Sources: Purpose and retention (fact-checked 2026-10-08)

- DPDP Act 2023, s.8(7) (erase on withdrawal or when it is reasonable to assume the purpose is no longer served, whichever is earlier, unless retention is necessary for compliance with law; cause processors to erase), s.8(8) (deemed no longer served after the prescribed period), s.8(11) (only contact initiated by the person counts).
- DPDP Rules 2025, Rule 8(1)–(2) and Third Schedule: e-commerce entities with ≥2 crore registered users in India, online gaming intermediaries with ≥50 lakh, social media intermediaries with ≥2 crore; three years from the latest of last approach, last exercise of rights, or commencement of the Rules; account access and stored-value virtual tokens carved out; intimation at least 48 hours before. Rule 8(3): personal data, traffic data and logs kept at least one year. In force from May 2027. The Rules don't say which commencement date starts the clock; the simulation uses 13 May 2027.
- Other retention laws: PMLA s.12 and the Maintenance of Records Rules 2005 (five years); CGST Act 2017 s.36 (72 months from the annual return due date); CERT-In Directions of 28 Apr 2022 (180-day logs). GST bar in the simulation is illustrative.
