- GDPR (Regulation (EU) 2016/679, EUR-Lex): Art. 3(2), 6(1), 8(1), 9, 20, 21, 22, 33, 34, 37, 83(4)–(6). EU Digital Omnibus GDPR amendments still under negotiation (Oct 2026); GDPR unchanged.
