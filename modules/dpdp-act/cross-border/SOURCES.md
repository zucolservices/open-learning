# Sources: Cross-border transfers (fact-checked 2026-10-08)

- DPDP Act 2023, s.16(1) (Central Government may restrict transfer to notified countries; none notified as of Oct 2026), s.16(2) (laws with a higher degree of protection or restriction not affected), s.17(1)(d) (offshore outsourcing carve-out), s.2(t). DPDP Rules 2025, Rule 15 (requirements for making data available to a foreign State; none issued), Rule 13(4) (SDF localisation of specified data; none specified). In force from May 2027.
- RBI circular DPSS.CO.OD.No.2785/06.08.005/2017-18 (6 Apr 2018) and FAQ (26 Jun 2019): payment data stored only in India; processing abroad allowed if deleted abroad and returned within one business day or 24 hours, whichever is earlier.
- IRDAI (Maintenance of Information by the Regulated Entities and Sharing of Information by the Authority) Regulations, 2025 (records in India; via secondary source); SEBI framework for adoption of cloud services (2023); MeitY cloud guidelines for government departments (2017); CERT-In FAQs Q35–Q36.
- Drafts: PDP Bill 2018 s.40 (serving copy), PDP Bill 2019 s.33 (sensitive stored in India; critical only in India), draft DPDP Bill 2022 s.17 (whitelist).
