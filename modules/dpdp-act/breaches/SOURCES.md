# Sources: Personal data breaches (fact-checked 2026-10-08)

- DPDP Act 2023, s.2(u) (breach definition incl. loss of access; confidentiality, integrity or availability), s.8(6) (intimate the Board and each affected Data Principal), Schedule items 1 and 2 (up to ₹250 crore for safeguards, ₹200 crore for breach notices). s.2 in force since 13 Nov 2025; s.8 from May 2027.
- DPDP Rules 2025, Rule 7(1)(a)–(e) (to each person, without delay, via user account or registered channel: description, nature, extent, timing; likely consequences; mitigation; safety measures; business contact), Rule 7(2)(a) (Board, without delay: incl. location and likely impact), Rule 7(2)(b)(i)–(vi) (detailed report within 72 hours or longer period the Board allows on written request). No materiality or encryption threshold. In force from May 2027.
- CERT-In Directions under IT Act s.70B(6), 28 Apr 2022: report Annexure I incidents (incl. data breach and data leak) within 6 hours of noticing; logs for 180 days; NTP sync; FAQs (partial reports; logs abroad if producible). No amendment found as of Oct 2026.
