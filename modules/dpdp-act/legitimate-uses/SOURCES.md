# Sources: Legitimate uses (fact-checked 2026-10-08)

- DPDP Act 2023, s.4(1) (consent or legitimate uses), s.2(d), s.7(a)–(i) with the pharmacy-receipt and real-estate-broker illustrations to 7(a) and the maternity-benefit illustration to 7(b); s.11(1) and s.12(1) (rights cover consent "including consent as referred to in clause (a) of section 7"); s.5(1) (notice tied to consent requests).
- DPDP Rules 2025, Rule 5 and the Second Schedule (standards for State processing under s.7(b) and s.17(2)(b), incl. item (g) intimation). In force from May 2027 (G.S.R. 843(E); Rule 1(4)).
- GDPR Art. 6(1) for the six lawful bases. No general "legitimate interests" or "contract" ground in the DPDP Act. Fraud scoring is treated as a grey area.
