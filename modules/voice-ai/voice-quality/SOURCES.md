# Sources: Testing and running voice agents (fact-checked 2026-10-05)

- WER definition (NIST sclite; standard ASR literature): (S + D + I) / N.
- Percentile reporting: Google SRE book, "Service Level Objectives".
- Contact-centre metrics: containment, transfer rate, average handling time (talk + hold + after-call work).
- Simulated-caller testing tools: Coval, Hamming, Cekura; Vapi, Retell and ElevenLabs testing features; OpenAI Evals with audio input. Examples only.
- US recording law: 18 U.S.C. §2511(2)(d) (one-party); California Penal Code §632 (all-party), plus other all-party states.
- GDPR Art. 6 (lawful basis), Art. 4(14) and Art. 9 (biometric data for unique identification).
- EU AI Act Art. 50(1), applies 2 Aug 2026.
- FCC Declaratory Ruling FCC 24-17 (Feb 2024): AI-generated voices are "artificial" under the TCPA.

Personas, test results and latencies are illustrative.
