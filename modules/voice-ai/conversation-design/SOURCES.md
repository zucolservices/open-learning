# Sources: Designing voice conversations (fact-checked 2026-10-05)

- Google Conversation Design guidelines (written for Google Assistant; Conversational Actions retired 13 Jun 2023): Confirmations (explicit vs implicit, one-step corrections, grounding) and Errors (No Input, No Match, escalating reprompts, "don't say 'I didn't hear you'", no more than 3 errors in a row).
- Cohen, Giangola & Balogh, _Voice User Interface Design_ (Addison-Wesley, 2004): background; not quoted.
- Twilio, Warm Transfer tutorial; OpenAI Realtime SIP guide (SIP REFER for transfers).
- Clutch survey via PR Newswire (11 Apr 2019): 501 US callers; 88% prefer a live agent to a phone menu; irrelevant options top complaint.
- EU AI Act Art. 50(1), applies from 2 Aug 2026 (Art. 113).
- California Bus. & Prof. Code §17941 (B.O.T. Act, operative 1 Jul 2019); AB 2905 (2024, effective 1 Jan 2025).
- FCC Declaratory Ruling FCC 24-17 (Feb 2024); NPRM FCC 24-84 (Aug 2024, proposal pending).
