# Sources: Sound as data (fact-checked 2026-10-05)

- C. E. Shannon, "Communication in the Presence of Noise", Proc. IRE, 1949 (sampling theorem).
- Red Book CD audio (IEC 60908): 44.1 kHz, 16-bit; ITU-T G.711 (8 kHz narrowband) and G.722 (16 kHz wideband), via standard references.
- A. Radford et al., "Robust Speech Recognition via Large-Scale Weak Supervision" (Whisper, 2022): 16 kHz, 80-channel log-Mel, 25 ms windows, 10 ms stride; large-v3 uses 128 Mel bins.
- S. S. Stevens, J. Volkmann, E. B. Newman, "A Scale for the Measurement of the Psychological Magnitude Pitch", JASA 8:185, 1937 (the 2595·log10 formula is a later fit, used here for drawing).
- Analog Devices MT-001 (≈6 dB per bit); National Center for Voice and Speech (typical speaking pitch).
- OpenAI Realtime and Gemini Live docs (24 kHz output audio).

The word "sun" is synthesised with simplified acoustics for teaching.
