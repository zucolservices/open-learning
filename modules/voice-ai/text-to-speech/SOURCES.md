# Sources: Speech synthesis (fact-checked 2026-10-05)

- DeepMind, "WaveNet: A generative model for raw audio" (8 Sep 2016): closed the gap to human speech by over 50% (US English, Mandarin).
- J. Shen et al., Tacotron 2 (Dec 2017): MOS 4.53 vs 4.58 for professionally recorded speech.
- C. Wang et al., VALL-E (Microsoft, Jan 2023): 3-second enrolment.
- Flow-matching and diffusion TTS: Voicebox (2023, withheld), Matcha-TTS (2023), F5-TTS (2024).
- Model cards: Kokoro-82M (Apache-2.0), Kyutai TTS (CC-BY 4.0, Jul 2025); vendor docs for ElevenLabs Flash (~75 ms) and Cartesia Sonic (sub-90 ms), model-only figures.
- ITU-T P.800 (1996) and P.800.1 (2016): MOS 1–5 scale.

Ratings and timings in the interactives are illustrative; the play button uses the browser's built-in voice.
