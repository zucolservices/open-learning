# Sources: Why voice is hard (fact-checked 2026-10-05)

- T. Stivers et al., "Universals and cultural variation in turn-taking in conversation", PNAS 106(26), 2009 (10 languages; yes/no questions; modal gap 0–200 ms).
- S. C. Levinson & F. Torreira, "Timing in turn-taking and its implications for processing models of language", Frontiers in Psychology 6:731, 2015 (~600 ms+ to plan a word; long gaps signal reluctance).
- OpenAI, "Hello GPT-4o" (13 May 2024): "as little as 232 milliseconds, with an average of 320 milliseconds"; previous Voice Mode 2.8 s (GPT-3.5) and 5.4 s (GPT-4); rollout dates July–Sept 2024.
- Daily/Pipecat voice agent guidance (~800 ms voice-to-voice target; vendor rule of thumb).
- B. H. Juang & L. R. Rabiner on AT&T's VRCP (1992); Apple newsroom (Siri, Oct 2011); Amazon Echo (Nov 2014).
- Kaiser Permanente / NEJM Catalyst (2025) on ambient AI scribes.

The "feel the gap" bands are a rough teaching guide.
