# Sources: Bias and fairness (fact-checked 2026-10-05)

- Parrish et al., "BBQ: A Hand-Built Bias Benchmark for Question Answering" (2022): nine categories, ~58,000 examples, ambiguous and disambiguated contexts.
- Tamkin et al. (Anthropic), "Evaluating and Mitigating Discrimination in Language Model Decisions" (Dec 2023): 70 scenarios; positive and negative discrimination; prompt-based mitigation.
- Bloomberg, "OpenAI's GPT Is a Recruiter's Dream Tool. Tests Show There's Racial Bias" (Mar 2024): GPT-3.5 and GPT-4, eight résumés, 1,000 rankings per role; gaps except GPT-4 retail manager.
- OpenAI, "Evaluating fairness in ChatGPT" (first-person fairness, Oct 2024): ~0.1% harmful stereotypes overall, up to ~1% in some domains for older models.
- Kleinberg, Mullainathan & Raghavan (2016); Chouldechova (2017): incompatibility of fairness criteria with differing base rates.

The CV, names and rates are illustrative; the phone-shop item is written in BBQ's style for this module.
