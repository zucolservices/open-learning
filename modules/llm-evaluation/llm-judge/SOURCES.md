# Sources: LLM as a judge (fact-checked 2026-10-05)

- Zheng et al., "Judging LLM-as-a-Judge with MT-Bench and Chatbot Arena" (NeurIPS 2023): single, pairwise and reference-guided grading; GPT-4 vs experts 85% vs human–human 81% on non-tie votes (~66% with ties); position, verbosity and self-enhancement biases; swapping positions and conservative ties.
- Wang et al., "Large Language Models are not Fair Evaluators" (2023): order swaps changed ChatGPT's verdicts on 66 of 80 questions.
- Panickssery et al., "LLM Evaluators Recognize and Favor Their Own Generations" (2024).
- Liu et al., "G-Eval" (2023).
- OpenAI evaluation best practices; Anthropic evaluation docs (test the grader; clear rubrics).

The 40 pairs and the judge's behaviour are a simulation for illustration.
