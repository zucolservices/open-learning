# Sources: Deciding what good means (fact-checked 2026-10-05)

- Anthropic, "Define success criteria and build evaluations" (platform.claude.com): criteria specific, measurable, achievable, relevant; examples (F1 ≥ 0.85 on 10,000 held-out posts; < 0.1% of 10,000 outputs flagged toxic); non-exhaustive dimensions (task fidelity, consistency, relevance and coherence, tone and style, privacy, context utilisation, latency, price).
- OpenAI, "Evaluation best practices": define objective, collect dataset, define metrics, run and compare, continuously evaluate.
- Marilyn Strathern, "'Improving ratings': audit in the British University system" (1997); Charles Goodhart (1975).
- Liang et al., "Holistic Evaluation of Language Models" (HELM, 2022): seven metrics.
- OpenAI, "Expanding on what we missed with sycophancy" (2 May 2025).

Criteria options, scores and prices are illustrative.
