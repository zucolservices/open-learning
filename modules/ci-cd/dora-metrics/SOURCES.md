- DORA metrics guide (dora.dev/guides/dora-metrics, updated Jan 2026): the five definitions as quoted; throughput (lead time, deployment frequency, failed deployment recovery time) and instability (change fail rate, deployment rework rate); "speed and stability are not tradeoffs. In fact, we see that the metrics are correlated for most teams. Top performers do well across all five metrics, and low performers do poorly."; pitfalls "Setting metrics as a goal", "Having one metric to rule them all", "Making disparate comparisons", "Having siloed ownership", "Competing".
- DORA metrics history: four variables from the 2014 study; MTTR renamed failed deployment recovery time (2023); deployment rework rate added (2024).
- 2024 Accelerate State of DevOps report: elite cluster (lead time under one day, on-demand deploys, 5% change fail rate, recovery under one hour); "The best teams are those that achieve elite improvement, not necessarily elite performance."
- 2025 DORA State of AI-assisted Software Development: seven team archetypes replace performance tiers; AI associated with higher throughput and higher instability.
- Goodhart's law in Marilyn Strathern's phrasing (1997).
- DORA Quick Check; GitLab DORA metrics (Ultimate).
