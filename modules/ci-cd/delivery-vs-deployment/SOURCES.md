# Sources (fact-checked 2026-10-03, before building)

Full notes: scratchpad `cicd/m12-facts.md` (raw pages in `cicd/m12/`).

- Humble & Farley, _Continuous Delivery_ (2010), ch. 5: the deployment pipeline is "an automated manifestation of your process for getting software from version control into the hands of your users."
- DORA, "Streamlining change approval": "no evidence was found to support the hypothesis that a more formal, external review process was associated with lower change fail rates"; recommends peer review plus automation. DORA metrics guide: change lead time is "the amount of time it takes for a change to go from committed to version control to deployed in production".
- UK FCA, "Implementing Technology Change" (5 Feb 2021): over 1m production changes from 2019; "firms that deployed smaller, more frequent releases had higher change success rates"; "CABs approved over 90% of the major changes they reviewed".
- Monzo, "How we deploy to production over 100 times a day" (May 2022).
- RBI Master Direction on IT Governance, Risk, Controls and Assurance Practices (7 Nov 2023, effective 1 Apr 2024), section 13: changes "applied/ implemented and reviewed in a secure and timely manner with necessary approvals".
- ITIL 4: standard changes are "low-risk, pre-authorized changes … implemented without needing additional authorization"; change authorities may be peer review or automated. ITIL Version 5 launched January 2026.
- Apple App Review: on average 90% of submissions reviewed in less than 24 hours.
- Meta Engineering, "Rapid release at massive scale" (2017): quasi-continuous release, "tens to hundreds of diffs every few hours", fully rolled out April 2017.
- The month of 100 changes, fault rates, lead times and recovery times are illustrative.
