# Sources: Turnaround time (fact-checked 2026-10-11)

- Google Engineering Practices, "Speed of Code Reviews": one business day maximum; don't interrupt focused work.
- Sadowski et al., "Modern Code Review: A Case Study at Google", ICSE-SEIP 2018: initial feedback under 1 h for small changes, ~5 h for very large; overall median under 4 h; median change 24 lines; Rigby & Bird comparisons (14.7–19.8 h Microsoft, 17.5 h AMD, 15.7 h Chrome OS).
- Engineering at Meta, "Move faster, wait less" (16 Nov 2022): Nudgebot −7% average Time In Review, −12% diffs waiting >3 days; P75 tracked; Eyeball Time guardrail.
