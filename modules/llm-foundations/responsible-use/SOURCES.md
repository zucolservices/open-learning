# Sources (fact-checked 2026-09-25)

- DPDP Act 2023 (prsindia.org PDF): s.6(1) consent "free, specific, informed, unconditional and unambiguous with a clear affirmative action", limited to data "necessary for such specified purpose"; s.6(4) withdrawal with comparable ease; s.5(3) notice in English or any Eighth Schedule language; s.7(b) State legitimate use for prescribed subsidies, benefits, services, certificates, licences or permits (prior consent to a State scheme, or data in a notified State database); s.8(7) erasure; ss.11–12 rights. "A pre-ticked box isn't a clear affirmative action" is our reading, not the Act's words.
- DPDP Rules notified 14 Nov 2025 (PIB); phased over 18 months, most duties from 14 May 2027.
- Dutch childcare benefits scandal: ~26,000 parents wrongly accused; the risk-classification model (2013–2019) used non-Dutch nationality as a risk indicator (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens 2020; Amnesty "Xenophobic Machines", 2021); Rutte cabinet resigned 15 Jan 2021.
- Robodebt Royal Commission final report, 7 Jul 2023: "a crude and cruel mechanism, neither fair nor legal"; ~470,000 wrongly issued debts.
- EU AI Act Annex III 5(a) (eligibility for essential public assistance benefits = high-risk); Reg (EU) 2026/1744 (in force 27 Jul 2026) moves Annex III duties to 2 Dec 2027; Arts 9, 12, 14; Art 14(4)(b) names "automation bias".
- India AI Governance Guidelines (MeitY, 5 Nov 2025): seven sutras, voluntary.
