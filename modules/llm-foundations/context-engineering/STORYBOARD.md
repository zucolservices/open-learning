# Context engineering: storyboard

1. **The consultant's desk** ⭐ (scroll story): a consultant who knows the world but only what's on the desk about you; everything on the desk is context; the desk has an edge (window, tokens, cost); clutter hides things; curating the desk is context engineering.
2. **Fill the window** (simulation): turn 61 of a support chat; toggle blocks (whole help centre 180k vs top-5 search 1.5k, full history 24k vs summary 2.6k, orders, tools) against a 128k window; fit, missing knowledge/history warnings, cost per request and per 10,000 requests at $2/M.
3. **Lost in the middle** ⭐ (research chart): Liu et al. Table 6 accuracy by gold-document position for four models, GPT-3.5 closed-book line (56.1%) and oracle (88.3%); Chroma Context Rot quote.
4. **More text, more mistakes** ⭐ (real runs): Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct asked to list every Sunday-closed area from 6, 12, 24 or 30 articles vs the top 5 from a keyword search; recall and wrong areas listed, with a real answer.
5. **Remembering a long conversation** (compare): keep everything / last 6 / summary + last 6 / memory store; tokens per turn chart and whether the turn-3 address survives (illustrative).
6. **Stable parts first** (rearrange): reorder blocks; cached prefix, cost per request and per day with 0.1× cached price.
7. **Where should it live?** (sort checkpoint): every request / fetch when needed / leave out.
8. **What to remember**.
