# Hallucinations: storyboard

1. **Fluent isn't the same as true** (real probabilities): quiz-contestant analogy; next-token distribution for a known fact (Bengaluru/Bangalore 97%) vs an unknowable one (Kirana Express founder, top token 7%), on an absolute scale.
2. **Ask it five times** ⭐ (real samples): five temperature-1 answers to three questions; agreement vs scatter (invented authors, founder, chapter title); SelfCheckGPT.
3. **Diagnose and fix** ⭐ (fix-the-problem): eight questions (common knowledge, company data, non-existent book, obscure statistic, post-training event) × three modes (just ask / allow "I don't know" / ground in sources) with real answers, verdicts and notes; counts of correct / declined / wrong.
4. **Why models guess** (explore): confidence and penalty sliders show when guessing beats abstaining; SimpleQA abstention vs error numbers (Kalai et al.).
5. **Match the fix to the cause** (sort checkpoint): ground / search tool / allow "I don't know".
