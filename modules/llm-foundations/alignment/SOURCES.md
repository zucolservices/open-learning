# Sources (fact-checked 2026-09-25)

- Ouyang et al., InstructGPT (arXiv 2203.02155, 2022): SFT → reward model trained on rankings → PPO. Bradley–Terry pairwise preference model as used for RLHF reward models.
- Rafailov et al., "Direct Preference Optimization" (arXiv 2305.18290, 2023). Bai et al., "Constitutional AI: Harmlessness from AI Feedback" (arXiv 2212.08073, 2022).
- Sharma et al., "Towards Understanding Sycophancy in Language Models" (arXiv 2310.13548, 2023): assistants consistently sycophantic; humans and preference models sometimes prefer convincing sycophantic answers to correct ones.
- OpenAI, "Sycophancy in GPT-4o" (Apr 2025): update shipped 25 Apr and rolled back 29 Apr 2025; attributed to over-weighting short-term thumbs-up/down feedback.
- The reward model in step 2 is real (fitted in the browser); the two crowds' underlying tastes are illustrative.
