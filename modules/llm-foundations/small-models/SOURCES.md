# Sources (fact-checked 2026-09-25)

- Hinton, Vinyals and Dean, "Distilling the Knowledge in a Neural Network" (arXiv 1503.02531, 2015): soft targets from a teacher.
- DeepSeek-AI, DeepSeek-R1 (arXiv 2501.12948, 2025): reasoning distilled into Qwen and Llama models. Gemma Team, Gemma 2 (arXiv 2408.00118, 2024): smaller models trained with knowledge distillation.
- Model sizes: all-MiniLM-L6-v2 22.7M; Gemma 3 270M (Google, 14 Aug 2025); Qwen2.5 0.5B/1.5B; Phi-4-mini 3.8B (Microsoft, Feb 2025, MIT); Llama 3.1 8B/70B; DeepSeek-V3 671B.
- Apple Intelligence Foundation Language Models Tech Report 2025 (arXiv 2507.13575): ~3B on-device model, Foundation Models framework. Google, Gemini Nano via ML Kit GenAI APIs (developer.android.com/ai/gemini-nano).
- Our experiment (Sep 2026): 200 invented support messages (4 queues × 10 phrasings × 5 variants); test set = 2 held-out phrasings per queue (40 messages). onnx-community Qwen2.5-0.5B/1.5B-Instruct and Phi-4-mini-instruct at 4-bit in Transformers.js v4 on an Apple M3 Pro CPU; label chosen by the highest first-token logit among the four queue names; "with examples" adds eight labelled examples to the system prompt. Student: Xenova/all-MiniLM-L6-v2 mean-pooled embeddings + softmax regression (NumPy), trained on the 160 training messages with the teacher's hard labels (70%), soft labels at temperature 2 (75%), or correct labels (97.5%). Cascade: soft-label student answers when its top probability ≥ threshold, otherwise the teacher's answer; average time = 2.2 ms + escalated share × 926 ms. Llama-3.2-3B-Instruct's 4-bit ONNX export answered "delivery" for nearly every message, so it was left out.
- Calibration card (added 2026-09-29): Guo et al., "On Calibration of Modern Neural Networks" (ICML 2017). TypeSafe AI, Jev launch blog and docs (15 September 2026): Choice, Score and Noul primitives; "Reinforcement Learning for Calibrated Decisions (RLCD)"; calibration "is measured across groups of predictions; it does not guarantee that an individual answer is correct"; closed, API-only. Independent calibration test (scienthoon, GitHub, September 2026, not peer-reviewed): expected calibration error 0.107 on 900 synthetic tickets, Choice and Score overconfident.
